Chika Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 22, 2026

Publisher: Meng Yang

Contact: mengyang0529@gmail.com

Chika is an offline-first Japanese learning app. Chika does not require a Chika account and does not operate advertising, first-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. AI providers may keep the operational metadata described below.

Information stored on your device

Vocabulary, review activity, chat history, examples, and preferences are stored in Chika's local SQLite database on your iPhone. API keys you enter in Settings are transferred to the iOS Keychain through Chika's local app bridge; once saved, they are isolated by provider in Keychain, read only by native code when dispatching requests to the selected provider, and never returned to the web interface or logged.

Local database upgrade backups maintain up to 5 rolling snapshots within the app container. When you remove Chika from your device, all local app container data (including SQLite files and upgrade backups) is deleted. You can delete individual conversations and vocabulary items, delete the selected provider's key, or delete all Chika API keys in one action inside Settings. Because iOS may retain Keychain items after app removal, use “Delete all saved keys” before uninstalling if you do not want them retained.

AI features and international data transfer

AI features are optional. Before the first AI request, Chika explains the data transfer and asks for your consent. When you choose AI chat, the text you submit, conversation context, and the selected provider's API key are sent directly from your device to its configured API address so that the provider can generate a response. Release builds include DeepSeek, OpenAI, and OpenRouter destinations only. The OpenRouter preset connects directly to https://openrouter.ai/api/v1. Chika does not operate an intermediary server for these requests.

When Apple Translation is unavailable, Chika may send bundled Chinese meanings or example translations to the selected AI provider to produce a translation in the app's display language. This fallback is used only after you save that provider's API key and accept AI data transfer. OpenRouter may route requests to a downstream model provider; retention and training behavior can vary with your OpenRouter privacy settings, ZDR routing, and the downstream provider. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

The selected provider and its infrastructure may also receive and retain network and request metadata, including your IP address, approximate location derived from IP, selected model, token usage, request timing, and abuse-prevention or diagnostic records. The exact fields and retention period depend on the provider, any downstream provider, and your account settings.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo-library access is optional and is used only when you choose image recognition. Chika performs OCR on the device. Chika does not send the selected image to an AI provider.

Tracking, advertising, and sale of data

Chika does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal data.

Changes, support, and contact

Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy's effective date. Questions, support requests, or reports about AI responses can be sent to mengyang0529@gmail.com.
